Why exactly 16 days?

For a Bay Area home under 2,500 sq ft, 16 days is the sweet spot. Just enough time to fix what turns buyers off, without taking on bigger problems.

It also leaves enough time for cleaning, inspections, staging, photos/videos, and pre-launch marketing.

Bigger prep jobs get riskier and return less per dollar. You don’t want to become a flipper. The Bay Area has some of the worst margins for flippers in the country. Many only aim for 7% profit. Not worth the risk.

For larger homes, the timeline grows: 19 days for 2,500+ sq ft, and 22 days above 3,000 sq ft.

I represented a Bay Area home buyer and the seller shared invoices for the prep work they did to get the house ready. They spent over $50,000. It was a waste.
