The Origin of the 16-Day Prep System

The $47k Waste

I represented a Bay Area home buyer and the seller shared invoices for the prep work they did to get the house ready. They spent over $50k. It was a waste.

My client was already planning a full renovation right after purchase, so roughly $47k of the seller's prep work was wasted. We gutted the kitchen cabinets they repainted, replaced the windows where they painted the trim, and tore out the floors they refinished.

The Seller's Dilemma

Shortly after, that same client asked me to list 1458 Portobelo. He was busy with his work and didn't know what to do anyway. I realized Bay Area sellers are usually stuck with three bad choices with traditional agents:

  • Sell As-Is: Leave money on the table.
  • Do It Yourself: Burn your weekends sourcing estimates and managing contractors.
  • Fake Concierge: A bait and switch with fake concierge agents that are actually just middlemen that pass you off to third parties like Revive or Curbio. Everyone takes a cut. They promise no up-front costs until closing, but they hide their profit in their heavily inflated prep costs. The incentives work against you because the contractor makes more money if they do more prep work on your home before the sale. I couldn't in good conscience offer this fake concierge service because it goes against my fiduciary duty to protect my clients' interests.

A Better Choice

I told my client upfront that if I took total ownership of the project, he had to let me use the property as a case study and show other homeowners the difference in how a sale should be executed. He agreed.

I took total ownership of the sale at 1458 Portobelo so my client only had to approve key decisions and pay invoices remotely. I developed the strategy, the scope of work, a projected budget, and a timeline. He approved, and the 16-Day Prep System was born.

Why Exactly 16 Days?

For a Bay Area home under 2,500 sq ft, 16 days is the sweet spot with a small built-in buffer. It's just enough time to execute the most common high-impact prep items that target buyer-perception-killing issues — without taking on unnecessary risk.

It also leaves enough time for cleaning, inspections, staging, photos/videos, and pre-launch marketing.

Larger and more costly prep work starts to increase execution risk and hits diminishing returns. As a homeowner you don't want to step into the role of a flipper. The Bay Area has some of the worst margins for flippers in the nation — many only targeting a 7% profit. The risk-reward just isn't worth it.

For larger homes, the prep system scales predictably: 19 days for 2,500+ sq ft, and 22 days above 3,000 sq ft.

The Bottom Line

The 16-Day Prep System was built so homeowners can get the most for their home without increased risk, wasted time, or stress.
