The 16DayPrep: what I did on a recent San Jose sale.

The homeowner signed up and handed me the keys to 1458 Portobelo Dr in San Jose.

16 days later, this home went from "needs work" to clean, move-in ready, and market-ready. I put the house on the market on day 17. The homeowner went under contract in 7 days.

A total of 24 days from when he handed me the keys to accepting an offer.

What I did

In 16 days.

  • Repaired damaged walls and ceilings.
  • Interior paint: walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and closets.
  • Exterior paint: stucco, trim, eaves, doors, and garage door.
  • Electrical: replaced all ceiling lights, outlets, and switches.
  • Electrical repairs: fixed broken outlets and broken recessed lights.
  • Installed new smoke and carbon monoxide detectors.
  • Fireplace: removed the gold cover, cleaned and painted the interior black.
  • Master bath: added new shower glass door.
  • Hall bath: reglazed bathtub.
  • Repairs: fixed fencing and rotted exterior wood.
  • Repaired 2 doors that did not open and close smoothly.
  • Removed old window screens.
  • Handled trash-out inside and out.
  • Deep cleaned the home.
  • Refreshed curb appeal with mulch, trimming, edging, and yard cleanup.
  • Cut back 2 trees and bushes in contact with the home.
  • Powerwashed the driveway, patios, and walkways.
  • Completed inspections.
  • Staged, photographed, and launched the listing.
  • Licensed trades: C-33 painter. C-10 electrician.

What I skipped

The return wasn’t worth it.

  • No kitchen remodel.
  • No bathroom remodel.
  • No new flooring.
  • No HVAC replacement.
  • No roof replacement.
  • No inspection repairs.
  • No termite fumigation.
  • No full renovation.
