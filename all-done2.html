The 16 Day Prep: What We Did

The seller handed me the keys.

16 days later, this San Jose home went from "needs work" to clean, move-in ready, and market-ready.

What we did:

  • Interior paint: walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and closets.
  • Exterior paint: stucco, trim, eaves, doors, and garage door.
  • Electrical: replaced ceiling lights, outlets, and switches. Repaired outlets and broken recessed lights.
  • Fireplace: removed the gold cover and painted the interior black.
  • Bathroom: added frameless shower glass and reglazed the tub.
  • Repairs: fixed fencing and rotted exterior wood.
  • Removed old window screens.
  • Handled trash-out inside and out.
  • Deep cleaned the home.
  • Refreshed curb appeal with mulch, trimming, edging, and yard cleanup.
  • Completed inspections.
  • Staged, photographed, and launched the listing.
  • Licensed trades: C-33 painter. C-10 electrician.

Prep work cost: $18,454.

What We Skipped: These wouldn't have paid off here.

  • No kitchen remodel.
  • No bathroom remodel.
  • No new flooring.
  • No HVAC replacement.
  • No roof replacement.
  • No inspection repairs.
  • No full renovation.

Why I created 16DayPrep.

I couldn’t believe the invoices I was seeing. The seller wasted $47k.

We replaced the cabinets they’d repainted, the windows where they’d painted the trim, and ripped out the floors they’d refinished.

I realized most homeowners have to choose between four broken options:

Sell As-Is

Do It Yourself

Months managing contractors and burning weekends.

Fake Concierge

A bait and switch. The “concierge” agents are middlemen that pass you off to third parties like Revive or Curbio. Everyone takes a cut.

No up-front costs sounds great. The catch: they hide their profit in heavily inflated prep costs.

The incentives work against you. They make more money if they do more prep.

Commodity Realtor

Anyone can list, but your profit suffers.

A Better Choice

I told my client that if I managed the full prep and sale, I wanted to use his property an example to show others a different way.
